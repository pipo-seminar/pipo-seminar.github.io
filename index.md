## About

PIPO (Privacy, Informal Proofs & Open problems) is a one-day seminar on differential private machine learning, held in Paris just before NeurIPS 2026. It's an informal occasion to meet, discuss proofs, and work on open problems together.

## Format

- **Four talks** by researchers working on privacy.
- **Two open problem sessions**, each opening with a short presentation followed by discussion in small groups.
- **A poster session** in the afternoon.
- **Lunch** is provided by the organizers.

## Code of conduct

PIPO follows the [NeurIPS Code of Conduct](https://neurips.cc/public/CodeOfConduct), and all participants are expected to comply with it. If you experience or witness any problem, please report it to [Edwige Cyffers](https://cyffers.fr/), in person during the event or by email.
