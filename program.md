## Open problem sessions

Each session starts with a short presentation of 2 open problems, related to the talks presented by the speakers. We then split into small groups to discuss it. The aim is to work on the problem together; no finished results are expected.

## Poster session

Everyone is welcome to bring a poster, including posters you are presenting at NeurIPS 2026. If you would like to present one, email us at [{{ site.contact_email }}](mailto:{{ site.contact_email }}) so we can plan the space.
